## Ecosystem alignment notes

- Nostr: main deliberately implements a **proprietary** private envelope over Nostr kinds 14/13/1059 that is explicitly *not* NIP-17/NIP-44 compatible (documented in `NostrProtocol.swift`). That's upstream's design decision — but note for any fork divergence: do not advertise NIP-17 compatibility; the kind-number reuse means real NIP-17 clients will see undecryptable gift-wraps. Keep the in-code disclaimer current if you change the envelope.
- NIP-13 PoW gating (`NostrPoW.rateLimitBypassBits`) and NIP-40 expirations on courier drops are current-practice Nostr — good.
